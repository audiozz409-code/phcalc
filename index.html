How Philippines Payroll Deductions Work

Every Filipino employee's salary is subject to mandatory government contributions before arriving at net pay.

  • SSS: Based on Monthly Salary Credit bracket, employee share ~4.5%
  • PhilHealth: 5% of monthly basic salary (split employer/employee), 2026 rate
  • Pag-IBIG: 1-2% of monthly compensation, capped contribution
  • Withholding Tax: Based on BIR graduated tax table (TRAIN Law)

BIR Tax Table 2026 (TRAIN Law)

  • ₱0 – ₱20,833: 0% (Tax Exempt)
  • ₱20,833 – ₱33,333: 15% of excess over ₱20,833
  • ₱33,333 – ₱66,667: ₱1,875 + 20% of excess over ₱33,333
  • ₱66,667 – ₱166,667: ₱8,541.80 + 25% of excess over ₱66,667
  • ₱166,667 – ₱666,667: ₱33,541.80 + 30% of excess over ₱166,667
  • Above ₱666,667: ₱183,541.80 + 35% of excess

13th Month Pay Rules in the Philippines

Under Presidential Decree No. 851, all rank-and-file employees who worked at least one month are entitled to 13th month pay, computed as total basic salary earned during the year divided by 12.

  • Must be paid on or before December 24 each year
  • 13th month pay up to ₱90,000 is tax-exempt
  • Amounts exceeding ₱90,000 are subject to tax on the excess
