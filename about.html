Free salary and tax calculator tools built specifically for the Philippines — accurate, fast, and mobile-friendly.

What is PHCalc?

PHCalc is a free online calculator platform built for Filipino employees and workers. Our tools reflect actual Philippine government mandated contributions and tax rules — SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG, and BIR withholding tax — all updated for 2026.

All tools are completely free. No registration required. Calculations happen instantly in your browser.

Why We Built PHCalc

Payroll calculations in the Philippines involve multiple mandatory government contributions that change periodically. PHCalc simplifies this by giving employees a clear, accurate breakdown of their take-home pay and statutory deductions.

Accuracy & Updates

Our calculators are based on:

  • Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR) TRAIN Law tax tables
  • Social Security System (SSS) contribution schedule 2026
  • PhilHealth premium rate 2026
  • Home Development Mutual Fund (Pag-IBIG) contribution table
  • Department of Labor and Employment (DOLE) overtime pay rules
